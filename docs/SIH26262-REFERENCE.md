# SIH26262 — Problem Statement Reference

**Official title:** Integrated AICTE Approval Workflow Automation, Compliance Monitoring and Alert Management System.  
**Theme:** Smart Automation · **Category:** Software · **Team:** Aayam · **Team ID:** 197726.

## Core problem

Manual follow-up across calls, emails and spreadsheets consumes officer time; pending cases are difficult to see across desks; and leases/undertakings can lapse without timely warnings.

## Expected solution themes

1. One engine for every approval and deadline.
2. Role-based access for institutions, officers and EVC committees.
3. Application/document intake and rule-driven APH workflow paths.
4. Parallel scrutiny, EVC, compliance-evidence and document-validity tracks.
5. Smart reminders (the idea deck specifies T-30 and T-7) and overdue escalation.
6. Dashboards and officer decisions with an auditable record.
7. AI-assisted document reading with an officer confirming extracted details.

## Expected impact

Less manual chasing, fewer missed deadlines, improved processing visibility, better compliance tracking and an audit-ready record of who did what and when.

## Regulatory boundary

AI assists; officers alone make approvals/rejections and other statutory decisions. The platform must follow current AICTE rules, the applicable APH and authorised administrative procedures.
